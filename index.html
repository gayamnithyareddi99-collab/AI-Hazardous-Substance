Core Intelligence Modules

Six Modules. One Complete AI Safety Platform.

AI Hazardous Substance combines Real-Time Spatial Mapping, Unified Compliance Graph, GAN Risk Intelligence, Automated Evidence Vault, Emergency Response Hub, and Edge AI Processing into one unified SaaS platform no-code, real-time, UK COSHH and RIDDOR compliant.

Module 01

Real-Time Spatial Compliance Mapping

AI-driven spatial mapping instantly converts physical warehouse layouts into digital, compliant storage maps with sub-200ms latency.

The Storage-Layout Compliance Mapping Engine uses hybrid CNN and Transformer architectures to detect chemical containers, bunding integrity, and spatial separation violations in real time. Unlike reactive safety tools, it interprets live human-material interactions and flags risks instantly covering oxidizers near flammables, acids near cyanides, and all COSHH incompatibilities automatically.
Module 02

Unified Compliance Graph (UCG)

A proprietary graph framework mapping thousands of chemical interactions against UK HSE mandates always governed by latest COSHH and REACH standards.

The UCG uses deep learning to map relationships between substance types, storage locations, and regulatory control measures. It automatically cross-references site-specific layouts against complex COSHH/HSE mandates ensuring correct ventilation, PPE, or spill kits are always linked to the correct storage location without requiring manual expertise.
Module 03

Generative Risk Learning Engine

GANs simulate rare hazard scenarios chemical leaks, lithium-ion thermal runaway creating adaptive intelligence that prevents accidents before they occur.

Our Generative Adversarial Networks continuously generate synthetic datasets of rare industrial risk events, addressing the long-standing data scarcity problem in safety-critical AI development. This proprietary data moat enables the AI to "practice" for high-impact events that cannot be safely replicated in real-world environments.

Everything you need to know about AI Hazardous Substance and what it means for your facility's COSHH compliance and worker safety in the age of industrial AI.

What is AI Hazardous Substance and why does my facility need it?

+
AI Hazardous Substance is an AI-based spatial safety platform that solves the systemic compliance crisis facing UK industrial SMEs specifically the "safety-management gap" where hazardous chemical storage, thermal risks, and regulatory evidence generation are disconnected from real-time operational realities. Unlike standard digital checklists or periodic HSE consultancy, AI Hazardous Substance establishes a "Live Spatial Safety Layer" that automatically monitors chemical storage compatibility, generates immutable compliance evidence, and alerts staff to hazards in real time. Operational testing has confirmed 92% detection accuracy and sub-200ms latency in industrial conditions.

How is this different from traditional HSE consultancy or EcoOnline/SafetyCulture?

+
Traditional HSE consultancy is prohibitively expensive (£500–£1,500 per day) and provides only periodic "snapshots" of compliance. Competitors like EcoOnline focus on database-driven SDS management, while SafetyCulture provides general digital checklists neither offers real-time spatial intelligence. AI Hazardous Substance operates as a live spatial monitoring layer using computer vision and the Unified Compliance Graph to actively detect incompatible chemical storage, thermal anomalies, and spatial violations in real time 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, at a fraction of the cost.

Does the system work offline in steel-clad warehouses with poor connectivity?

+
Yes. A core design principle of AI Hazardous Substance is offline-first Edge AI processing. Using NVIDIA Jetson Orin hardware, the system performs full spatial-to-compliance inference locally without cloud connectivity. This ensures continuous safety monitoring in steel-clad logistics hubs, remote manufacturing units, and basement clinical maintenance zones. Site layouts and chemical inventory data remain encrypted and processed on-device providing both operational continuity and complete data sovereignty.

What UK regulations does the platform comply with?

+
AI Hazardous Substance is architecturally aligned with UK COSHH (Control of Substances Hazardous to Health) Regulations, RIDDOR (Reporting of Injuries, Diseases and Dangerous Occurrences Regulations), the Health and Safety at Work Act 1974, UK GDPR, NHS DTAC (Digital Technology Assessment Criteria) for healthcare deployments, UKCA/CE certification standards, ISO 45001 risk management, and ISO/IEC 27001 cybersecurity. The platform also aligns with the HSE's 10-year "health-first" enforcement strategy making it the most comprehensively UK-regulated AI safety platform in the sector.

What sectors and facility types does AI Hazardous Substance serve?

+
AI Hazardous Substance serves UK SME warehouses, logistics and distribution hubs, NHS maintenance trusts and clinical facilities, food production units, automotive workshops, light manufacturing plants, and university research laboratories. The platform features sector-specific modules the Medical Maintenance Module for clinical chemicals and bio-hazards, the Lithium-Ion Logistics Module for bulk battery storage and thermal runaway prevention, and the Food Production Module for cleaning agent compliance. Each module includes tailored chemical interaction graphs for that sector's unique hazards.

What is the "Evidence Vault" and how does it protect my business during HSE audits?

The Evidence Vault is an automated, tamper-proof digital record system that converts every physical inspection, chemical storage change, and safety training session into a time-stamped, geofenced, and immutable compliance log. During an HSE audit or RIDDOR incident investigation, businesses can instantly produce a complete, legally defensible audit trail without manual paperwork scrambling. Given that HSE health and safety fines averaged over £32,000 per case in 2024, the Evidence Vault pays for itself through fine prevention alone while also reducing insurance premiums for facilities with documented compliance histories.
